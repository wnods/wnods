<h1 align="center"> Hey, I'm Will.</h1>
<p align="left">I am a graduate student in Geophysics at the Federal University of Pará (UFPA), working at the Amazon Sustainable Petroseismics Laboratory (LPSA). My research focuses on the modeling and simulation of fluid dynamics in complex geological media.

My recent work has involved investigating acid injection into carbonate rock samples using micro-computed tomography (micro-CT), finite-volume computational fluid dynamics simulations with OpenFOAM, and the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM). These studies include the analysis of fluid velocity fields in porous rocks and the development of hybrid LBM-based approaches for simulating reactive transport and carbonate acidification.

I am also exploring Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for adaptive mesh refinement in single-phase and multiphase flow simulations, as well as conducting stability, validation, and convergence studies for numerical fluid-flow models in complex porous media.
